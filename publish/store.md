## Namn
Snuffle Pigs

## Summary (en rad)
Five hand-made pigs that smell ore through rock, root up truffles and carry you.

## Description

Minecraft already has a pig. It exists to become pork, and riding it with a
carrot on a stick barely steers. This gives the pig a job.

**A nose that finds things.** Put a pig in Snuffle mode and it senses ore through
the stone around it, then tells you what it smells, which way it lies and how far
off it is. A compass direction and a distance — not exact coordinates. It tells
you where to dig and leaves the digging to you.

**Truffles.** In Snuffle mode a pig roots truffles out of grass, dirt, podzol,
mycelium, moss and mud. You see the truffle in its snout for a moment before it
drops, so you can tell where it came from. Truffles are food, and they are also
the best breeding feed.

**Riding that works.** A saddle makes a pig steer the way a horse steers, with no
carrot on a stick involved. A chest adds saddlebags.

**Five breeds that differ in what they can DO.** Soot the Berkshire smells ore
twelve blocks away; Blossom the Large White only five, but she carries the most
and takes the most punishment; Ember the Tamworth is the one you ride. You pick a
pig for the job, not for the colour.

They are not reskins. Each body is built from its own measurements, so the
Kunekune really is low and round and the Large White really is heavy.

**Also:** commands (Follow / Snuffle / Stay) with a stick, taming with carrots,
piglets that are born yours and grow up, and English and Swedish throughout.
