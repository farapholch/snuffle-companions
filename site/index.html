Snuffle Pigs

Five hand-made pigs for Minecraft Bedrock

Minecraft already has a pig. It exists to become pork, and riding it with a carrot on a stick barely steers. This gives the pig a job: it smells ore through solid rock and tells you which way it lies, it roots up truffles out of the ground, and saddled it actually steers.

__NEDLADDNING__

What they do

  • Catch a scent. Put a pig in Snuffle mode and it senses ore through the stone around it, then tells you what it smells, which way, and how far. A compass direction and a distance — not exact coordinates. It tells you where to dig and leaves the digging to you.
  • Root up truffles. In Snuffle mode a pig digs truffles out of grass, dirt, podzol, mycelium, moss and mud. You see the truffle in its snout for a moment before it drops, so you can tell where it came from.
  • Carry you properly. A saddle makes a pig rideable with full steering, the way a horse steers. Add a chest for saddlebags.
  • Take commands. Right-click with a stick to cycle Follow, Snuffle and Stay.
  • Have piglets. Feed two tame pigs of the same breed a truffle, a potato or a beetroot and you get a piglet, born already yours. It grows up.

Truffles are food, and they are also the best breeding feed — what you find has a use even when the larder is full.

The five

The differences are not decoration. Soot smells ore more than twice as far as Blossom; Blossom carries the most and takes the most punishment; Ember is the one you ride. You pick a pig for the job, not for the colour.

Nose is how many blocks of stone a pig can smell through. Tame any of them with a carrot — about four tries in ten per carrot.

They are not reskins. Each body is built from its own measurements, so the Kunekune really is low and round and the Large White really is heavy. What makes a pig read as a pig at a glance is the snout, so every breed has its own.
